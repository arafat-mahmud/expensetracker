# Quick Start Guide - Debit/Credit System

## 🎯 New Features at a Glance

Your expense tracker now supports **complete financial management** with income tracking, balance monitoring, and statement downloads!

## 📱 Using the New Features

### 1. Adding Income (Credit)

**From Dashboard:**
1. Look for the **green "Add Income"** button at the bottom right
2. Tap it to open the income entry form

**Fill in the details:**
- **Category**: Choose from Salary, Business, Freelance, Investment, Gift, or Other
- **Amount**: Enter the income amount
- **Date**: Select when you received the income
- **Title** (optional): Add a custom description
- **Note** (optional): Add any additional details

3. Tap **"Save Income"**
4. Your balance will update automatically!

### 2. Viewing Your Balance

**On Dashboard (Top Card):**
- **Current Balance**: Shows your total income minus expenses for the month
- **Income**: Total money received (green)
- **Expense**: Total money spent (red)
- Card color changes based on whether you're in profit (green) or deficit (red)

**Navigate months:**
- Use the arrows at the top to view different months
- Balance is calculated per month

### 3. Managing Transactions

**View All Transactions:**
1. Tap **"History"** in the bottom navigation
2. See all your income and expenses in one place

**Filter by Type:**
- Tap **"All"** to see everything
- Tap **"Expenses"** to see only debits (money out)
- Tap **"Income"** to see only credits (money in)

**Filter by Category:**
- Scroll through category chips below the type filter
- Tap any category to filter transactions

**Edit or Delete:**
- Tap any transaction card
- Use the **edit** icon to modify
- Use the **trash** icon to delete

### 4. Downloading Statements

**Access Statement Page:**
- **Option 1**: Settings → "Download Statement"
- **Option 2**: History page → Download icon (top right)

**Select Period:**

*Quick Select (recommended):*
- Last 30 Days
- This Month
- Last Month
- Last 3 Months
- Last 6 Months
- This Year

*Custom Range:*
- Tap "Start Date" to choose beginning date
- Tap "End Date" to choose ending date

**Preview Summary:**
- Review transaction count
- Check total income and expenses
- Verify balance before downloading

**Download:**
- **PDF Statement**: Professional format, ready to print or email
  - Tap "Download PDF Statement"
  - Choose to share or save
  
- **CSV Statement**: Spreadsheet format for Excel/Sheets
  - Tap "Download CSV Statement"
  - Import into spreadsheet apps

## 💡 Pro Tips

### Best Practices:

1. **Record Income Immediately**
   - Add salary as soon as you receive it
   - Log freelance payments when they arrive
   - Don't forget gifts and other income sources

2. **Check Balance Regularly**
   - Review the dashboard balance card daily
   - Compare against your budget
   - Watch for negative balances

3. **Use Categories Wisely**
   - Salary: Regular employment income
   - Business: Business revenue
   - Freelance: Project-based earnings
   - Investment: Dividends, interest, capital gains
   - Gift: Money received as gifts
   - Other: Everything else

4. **Download Statements**
   - Monthly for personal tracking
   - Quarterly for tax preparation
   - Yearly for annual reviews
   - Keep PDF copies for records

5. **Statement Uses**
   - Tax filing documentation
   - Budget planning
   - Financial goal tracking
   - Sharing with accountant
   - Personal finance analysis in Excel

## ❓ Common Questions

**Q: Will my old expenses still show?**
A: Yes! All existing expenses are automatically categorized as debits.

**Q: Can I edit income entries?**
A: Yes, tap the income in History, then use the edit icon.

**Q: What format is the PDF statement?**
A: Professional A4 format with summary and detailed transaction table.

**Q: Can I open CSV files on my phone?**
A: Yes, use Google Sheets, Excel, or any spreadsheet app.

**Q: Does balance include previous months?**
A: The dashboard shows current month balance. For overall balance, download a statement covering all time.

**Q: Can I have negative balance?**
A: Yes, if expenses exceed income, the balance will be negative (shown in red).
